About Catering


Tradition since 1889

The Tail of the Dragon is one of the most famous driving roads in the United States, featuring 318 curves in just 11 miles. Located on the border of North Carolina and Tennessee, it attracts car enthusiasts, motorcyclists, and thrill seekers from around the world.

Our Menu


318 Curves in 11 Miles

The Tail of the Dragon is famous for its 318 tight turns packed into just 11 miles, making it one of the most technical driving roads in the country.


Scenic Mountain Views

Surrounded by the Great Smoky Mountains, the road offers incredible views, dense forests, and a true backcountry driving experience.


Popular with Enthusiasts

Drivers, motorcyclists, and car clubs from all over the country come here to test their skills and enjoy the thrill of the drive.


Photography & Souvenirs

Professional photographers capture action shots along the route, giving visitors a chance to take home memories of their ride.


Safety First

With no intersections or driveways, the focus is entirely on driving but caution is key, as the road’s curves demand full attention.
